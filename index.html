Little did he know that Allah was about to make that ordinary trip one of the most unforgettable chapters of his life. 🥹🫶🏻

A day that might have seemed like an ordinary day to the rest of the world, but became something extraordinary for us. Because that was the day a certain girl named Malaika caught Hassan's attention. And OH MY GOD, THE MAXI LOOK! 😭😂❤️

I swear, if someone had told me that one look at a girl wearing a maxi would turn into a love story that would mean this much to me, I probably would have laughed. But look at us now!
